Optional values to add to your existing backend/.env (DO NOT replace its SECRET_KEY):
SMTP_HOST=smtp.example.com
SMTP_PORT=587
SMTP_TLS=true
SMTP_USER=your-smtp-user
SMTP_PASSWORD=your-smtp-password
SMTP_FROM=helpdesk@example.com

No SMTP settings are required for in-app alerts. Never commit real credentials.
